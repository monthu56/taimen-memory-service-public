What your team does with it

The memory is the foundation — verifiable context about your people, decisions and processes. It powers assistants and agents, but a human always reviews, decides and signs.

1

Your personal assistant

At your own workstation, ask about a policy, a past decision, or who owns a project. You get the answer with its exact source and the people behind it — you read it and decide.

2

Agents on the same memory

Process agents work alongside your team with their own roles and access. A tendering agent knows every tender and your company and drafts the strongest bid; a records agent assembles the paperwork. You review and sign.

3

Experience that stays

Every decision, document and expert is retained and searchable. When someone leaves, their experience stays — and a new hire gets sourced answers from day one.
